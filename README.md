## Introduction

The Focus Group is invited to take note of the following documents which apply to ITU's standardization activities:

1. **[Common Patent Policy for ITU-T/ITU-R/ISO/IEC](http://www.itu.int/en/ITU-T/ipr/Pages/policy.aspx)**

   This document encourages the early disclosure and identification of patents that may relate to ITU Recommendations under development. Early disclosure of standard-essential patents encourages greater efficiency in standards development and limits potential patent rights problems.

2. **[Guidelines for Implementation of the Common Patent Policy for ITU-T/ITU-R/ISO/IEC](http://www.itu.int/oth/T0404000001/en)**

   This document is intended to clarify and facilitate implementation of the above-mentioned common Patent Policy. It lists actions to be taken by any party participating in the work of ITU, including, in particular, patent holders.

3. **[ITU Software Copyright Guidelines](http://www.itu.int/oth/T0404000004/en)**

   This document provides guidance concerning the incorporation of material protected by copyright law in ITU Recommendations and lists actions to be taken should a technical group decide to include copyrighted software in ITU Recommendations.

4. **[ITU Guidelines related to the inclusion of Marks](http://www.itu.int/oth/T0404000006/en)**

   This document provides guidance concerning the use of trademarks, service marks and certification marks in ITU Recommendations and lists actions to be taken should a technical group decide to reference any such marks.

## Provisions of Recommendation ITU-T A.1

By way of reminder, the following provisions of [Recommendation ITU-T A.1](http://itu.int/ITU-T/A.1) must also be followed by Focus Groups:

> <ins>**A.1 section 1.4.6:**</ins> "*Chair will ask, during each meeting, whether anyone has knowledge of intellectual property rights issues, including patents, copyright for software or text, marks, the use of which may be required to implement or publish the Recommendation being considered. The fact that the question was asked shall be recorded in the working party or study group meeting report [or FG meeting], along with any affirmative responses.*"

> <ins>**A.1 section 3.1.6:**</ins> "*By making a contribution, contributors acknowledge, to the best of their knowledge, that material such as text, diagrams, etc., submitted as their contribution to the work of ITU‑T has no restriction in order to permit the normal distribution of this material for discussions within the appropriate ITU-T study groups and other groups and possible use, in whole or in part, with or without modification, in any resulting ITU-T Recommendations that are published (see [PP Res. 66]).*"

> <ins>**A.1 section 3.1.8:**</ins> "*A contributor submitting copyrighted software for incorporation in the draft Recommendation is required to submit a software copyright statement and licensing declaration form available at the ITU‑T website. The form must be provided to TSB at the same time that the contributor submits the copyrighted software<sup>1</sup>.*"
